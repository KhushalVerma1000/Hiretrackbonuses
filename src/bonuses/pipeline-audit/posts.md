## LinkedIn
Where is your hiring pipeline stuck?

Most agencies can tell you how many candidates they have. Fewer can tell you how many have been waiting too long, and for whom.

A 2025 survey of 824 candidates in Ireland found 28% had dropped out of a hiring process. Of those, 22% said it took too long and 21% said communication was unclear or slow. On the recruiter side (475 recruiters), 26% said follow-up after interviews slows hiring and 19% said gathering feedback from hiring managers does.

We built a free Pipeline Audit. You enter, for six stages: candidates waiting, average days, longest wait and your target.

You get:
• the bottleneck stage (most candidate-days over target)
• who the pipeline is waiting on: your team, the client or candidates
• the candidates most at risk of going cold
• three fixes for the bottleneck and a check of six process habits
• a CSV and a printable report

Any country, about three minutes. A planning aid, not a verdict.
