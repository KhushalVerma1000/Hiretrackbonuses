## LinkedIn
Which of your candidates is about to go quiet?

LiveCareer's 2026 survey of 1,008 US workers found that 1 in 4 job seekers have ghosted an employer. The most common reason was accepting another job (51% of those who ghosted). Ghosting also happened after interviews (13%) and after offers (8%), not only after applications.

The warning signs usually show up days earlier: slow replies on either side, no agreed next step, pay not confirmed, or a competing offer.

So we built a free Candidate Ghosting Risk Score. Nine questions per candidate give you:
• a 0 to 100 risk score
• the three biggest reasons behind it
• what to do and a message draft for each reason
• a ranked shortlist of up to 10 candidates, with CSV and print

It uses only process signals, never personal traits, and it is a prompt for follow-up, not a reason to reject anyone.
